# Interview One-Pager

## 30-second explanation
I used a Lean Six Sigma DMAIC framework to analyze a global proposal process with excessive cycle time. I translated stakeholder complaints into CTQs, established baseline cycle-time/SLA metrics, segmented performance across business dimensions, investigated process-step bottlenecks, and developed improvement and control recommendations.

## Best business story
The case starts with a 15% cycle-time mandate, but the analysis does not jump directly to solutions. It first defines the customer/process requirements, establishes the baseline, then tests where variation is concentrated.

## Best technical story
The course combines process analytics with SPC, capability analysis and DOE. I can explain when a control chart is appropriate, why capability and stability are different questions, and how factorial experiments distinguish main effects from interactions.

## Resume connection
This complements professional healthcare operational reporting and supply-chain analytics experience: the portfolio project demonstrates the formal statistical/process-improvement methodology behind the same broader goal—turning operational variation into action.
