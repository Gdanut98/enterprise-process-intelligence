# Source-Derived Case Evidence

## Executive mandate
- Revenue decline: 18% over two years
- Enterprise scale: ~$60B annual revenue
- Presence: ~100 countries
- Workforce: >150,000 employees/contractors
- Leadership cycle-time target: 15% reduction

## Process
Seller → ERP request → Bid Support Specialist routing → completeness check → manager review → Product Design → seller approval → Pricing → Brand approval → final proposal review → Seller.

The case explicitly identifies:
- non-standard practices,
- unnecessary approvals,
- inefficient/redundant tasks,
- slow brand approval and pricing,
- incomplete seller information,
- bid-support experience differences,
- identical approval paths regardless of deal size/complexity,
- multiple handoffs.

## SLA definition
A proposal taking more than 35 days is considered a defect.

## Analytical requirements from the case
- establish a project charter,
- build a detailed swimlane process map,
- superimpose average step and end-to-end times,
- calculate DPMO and sigma level,
- compare brands/geographies,
- analyze timestamps for root causes,
- compare seller/BSS performance,
- investigate relationships among process variables,
- recommend improvements using DMAIC.
